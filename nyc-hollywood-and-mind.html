A Daily Desk at WeWork

430 Park Ave, New York, NY 10022 · community team on the 5th floor

E and M trains at 5th Ave/53 St are a 4 minute walk. Buses stop at Madison Ave and E 54 St, 3 minutes.

An All Access membership books one desk a day at hundreds of WeWork locations, so a travel day still gets real work done. Pantry, lounges and phone booths are included. Meeting rooms are a separate booking. I worked for hours, then changed for the evening right there.
