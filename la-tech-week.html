Getting around

Transit or car: both are listed at every stop. I ran the week car-free because Westside parking during Tech Week is close to impossible, but if you drive, the numbers are there too.
Metro fares cap at $5 a day and $18 for any 7 days with TAP. The whole week of travel costs at most $18.
The E Line links Santa Monica, the Westside, Culver City and Downtown. Buses cover Venice, the Marina, Playa Vista and South LA.
Driving instead? Every stop also shows the drive: miles, time, gas at about 20 cents a mile, and a typical parking range for that neighbourhood. Parking is the real cost: Santa Monica, Venice, Downtown and Hollywood run about $10 to $25 a visit during big events, while Windsor Hills and South LA usually have free street parking. Park once per neighbourhood and walk. Check SpotHero or ParkWhiz for live prices and to reserve a spot.
How the numbers work. Distances are approximate miles between neighbourhoods. Transit times include waiting; drive times assume no rush hour (4pm to 7pm can double them). Leave times are set for transit, so drivers have a little more slack. Check Google Maps or the Transit app before each hop.
