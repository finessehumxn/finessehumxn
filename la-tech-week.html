Getting around

One neighbourhood per half day. Santa Monica, Venice, Marina del Rey, Playa Vista and Culver City are one lane. Downtown is another. Crossing town between 4pm and 7pm costs an hour you don’t get back.
Metro E Line. $1.75 a ride with TAP, linking Santa Monica, Culver City and Downtown.
Uber or Lyft. Short Westside hops run roughly $12 to $25. These are estimates, so check the app.
Parking. Santa Monica and Venice fill fast in the evening. Park once per neighbourhood and walk between rooms.
